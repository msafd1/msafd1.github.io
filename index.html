Electromyography forearm sleeve

In progress

Texas Instruments ADS1299, ESP32-S3, custom printed circuit board, Meta Quest 3, force-sensitive resistors

A wearable that predicts finger pose and fingertip force from forearm muscle signals, to capture dexterous hand data for training robots.

  • 32-channel surface electromyography acquisition from four ADS1299 analogue front ends on a custom printed circuit board.
  • ESP32-S3 reads the chips and streams the data over Wi-Fi.
  • Ground truth comes from Meta Quest 3 hand tracking for finger pose, and fingertip force-sensitive resistors for pressure.
  • Battery-powered for isolation from mains power. Starting with gel snap electrodes and moving to dry electrodes.
